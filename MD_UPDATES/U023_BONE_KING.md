## The Bone King
- The Skeleton's model, 6 m tall (a Skeleton is 1.9 m). A boss: never flinches, more damage while it reels. It rises
  out of the portal in its room's show and roars as it takes its stance. Its name: THE BONE KING.
- Floor 3's boss (the Dragon stood in), and floors 4–7's (they have floor 3's enemies).

## Two lives (phase 2)
- One bar, lighter than the Dragon's: 1000 a life (the Dragon 1600).
- Brought down the first time, it falls to pieces like its skeletons and lies there 3 s; nothing hurts it. Then it
  rebuilds over 3 s, its bar filling again, and roars: its second life (phase 2), its pauses between moves shorter.
- Brought down in its second life, it dies: it crumbles and its bones burst; its chest rises by the wooden gate.

## Its moves (each warned; picked by how far you are)
- **Thrown skeletons:** it reaches down (0.8 s) and throws 3 skeletons, one every 0.6 s, each at where you stand as it
  throws. Each one's landing glows on the floor while it flies (1 s); landing, it smashes into bones: 16 and thrown
  back within 1.8 m. Keep moving.
- **Jump-smash:** it crouches (0.6 s) and jumps for where you stand (0.9 s in the air, its landing glowing on the
  floor), smashes (24 within 4.5 m of its body, a quake ring rolling 13 m: jump it), crouches 0.4 s and jumps again for
  where you are then, smashes again.
- **The grab** (close by: within 3.5 m of its body, in front of it): it reaches (0.55 s); caught, you're held up in
  front of its face and the world holds still. HIT!: 10 hits (attack presses) in 3.2 s and you break free (it reels
  2 s, hits do more; each of your hits 10). Too few and it throws you at the nearest wall: 30.
  - On screen: HIT! and your attack's key, the combo count (×N) slamming in with each hit, ten pips lighting up, the
    time draining (crimson when low); then BROKE FREE (gold) or THROWN (crimson).
- **Phase 2, once:** its second life opens with 3 skeletons called up out of the floor round it (never right by you);
  they rise as a fallen Skeleton rebuilds and fight as any Skeleton, their revive and all.
- **Phase 2 at 25% of its health or less, once: the final blast.** It stands and charges 3 s; the floor trembles more
  and more; its reach shows on the floor (a ring, and a disc filling it as it charges). Its reach covers half the
  room's floor, round it. Inside when it goes off: 60 and thrown far. The bar's mark sits at 25% in its second life.
