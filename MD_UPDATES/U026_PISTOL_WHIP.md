# Update 0.2.6 — the Pistol shoots straight; right click strikes with it

Status: built (2026-10-30). The designer:
- "I am noticing that the bullets is trying to be realistic and not shooting straight where I am aiming? it is straight
  when I am aiming in with right click"
- "With only pistol, right click is only a hit with the gun, like a close range gun hit with it, like we are punching,
  no aiming"
- "and to compensate the right click aim, the bullet is going straight toward the crosshair of the screen where we are
  aiming"

## Shots
- Every bullet goes exactly along your look, to the crosshair: no spread, no bloom, shot after shot.
- The view no longer kicks up with a shot (it moved the crosshair off where the bullets go); the gun still recoils in
  your hand.

## Right click: a pistol-whip
- A strike with the gun at a fist's reach, landing as a finisher punch does: **10** (a finisher's), knocked back (**8**
  m/s), its hit-stop and sparks. Held, it strikes again every **0.5 s**. It spends no bullet.
- The arm: drawn back a hair, then thrust out like a jab with the gun turned on its side (its frame leads), held a
  moment, and back (0.32 s; it lands 0.13 s in). The wrist stays nearly straight (22° at the blow).
- Not while it reloads; no shot while it swings (a click then fires as the swing ends). Spent (no bullets left), right
  click still strikes; a click throws it.

## Gone
- Aiming down the sights: its pose, the narrowed view and arms' lens, the slowed mouse, the steadied arms, the
  crosshair fading. F1 → Pistol loses the spread, bloom and aiming knobs and gains Pistol whip damage / knock / rate.
