## The Big Ninja (floor 3's mini boss)
- The leaper's execution (the leaper is drawn as the Ninja too): pulled in, the arm on your right torn off by your
  right fist, the other chopped off by your left hand, punched in the face left, right, and a last right that sends
  it flying.
- It's 2.6 m tall (the leaper 1.9 m), so it stands further off (1.7 m, the leaper 1.3 m) and the view is raised, so it's
  framed as the leaper is. The same timings, your hands the same moves.

## The Mushnub
- The slime's: the ground slam's clasped fists come down on its head; it's left flat.

## The Skeleton: telekinesis
- It's pulled in front of you (2.1 m) and lifted off the floor (0.55 m), shaking harder as it rises.
- Your right hand reaches out, open, its palm to it (low on your right, so it shows beside your hand); your left stays
  in its guard. Your hand trembles as you hold it, then your fist closes.
- As your fist closes it bursts into its bones where it hangs: each arm and leg in two, its head, its ribs, its hips.
- One shot: an executed Skeleton never rebuilds. A Skeleton lying in pieces can't be executed (it starts standing);
  hitting its bones still finishes it.
- Its sounds: the crush blow's and the burst's (no new sound).

## One more room every 2 floors
- Fight rooms on the way to the boss: 5 on floors 1–2, 6 on 3–4, 7 on 5–6, 8 on floor 7 (F1 → Rooms → Rooms on the way,
  One more room every). The shop and the challenge room stay off the way, as before.
- Each room deeper still sends 3 more enemies, so floor 7's 8th room has 35.

## The run ends after floor 7
- Floor 7's way down (after its boss) fades to black and shows the death screen's twin: "Run complete — Survived" in
  gold, the floor, the time, your kills; Retry (a new run) or Menu.
