# Update 0.3.15 — Skull Cove's crew (floor 6's enemies and mini bosses)

Status: built (2026-11-02). The plan's update 2 (`MD/FLOOR6_PIRATE_PLAN.md`; the designer: "Go with it", then "go"):
seven enemies and two mini bosses, none like another floor's. Floor 6 sends them now instead of the stand-ins. Its boss
still stands in until update 3 (the Big Shark under water).

## The crew
Models: the Pirate Kit's characters (Anne, Henry, Sharky, the Skeleton and the Skeleton_Headless, Mako, Captain
Barbarossa), its Tentacle, and two fliers no floor used (the Pigeon, the Bat). Their own colours (the kit's palette).

1. **Corsair** (Anne, her double axe). At range (5–15 m) she twirls her boarding hook over her head (0.8 s), then
   throws it straight at where you stand. If it catches you, you're **dragged to her** (16 m/s, up to 1.4 s) and her
   axe falls at once. Step aside from the throw and it misses (then she waits 5 s to throw again). Up close, her axe
   (18).
2. **Shanty Man** (Henry, his lute). He stands off and sings: a note every 1.2 s, and the crew within 12 m of him are
   **quickened** (everything they do ×1.35) while his song lasts. Come within 9 m and he backs off; corner him and
   he swings his lute (10). Kill him first.
3. **Bomber** (Sharky, his cleaver). At range (4.5–16 m) he lobs a **lit powder bomb** where you're going: **he leads
   you** (70% of where your run takes you while it flies, 4 m at most; never into a wall or the sea), so running
   straight meets it and a turn beats it. It lands,
   burns its fuse (1.4 s: its ring fills), then blows (2.8 m: 22 to you, 90 to enemies; it lights kegs). **Punch a
   bomb** (in the air or burning at your feet) and it flies back at the nearest Bomber and blows where it lands. Up
   close, his cleaver (16).
4. **Cursed Crewman** (the Skeleton, a cutlass). Brought down, it doesn't die: **its skull flies off** and it goes on
   **headless** (the Skeleton_Headless, its own health, 25). Headless it's blind: it charges where it last heard you (a
   jump, a landing, a dash, a slide, a slam, a punch, a kill) and swings wild there (18), or at you if you're right
   there. Stand still and quiet and it wanders. Kill it again.
5. **Thieving Gull** (the Pigeon). It circles high, hovers a moment (its warning), then swoops through where your chest
   was. If it catches you it **snatches 15 of your coins** and flies off with them: kill it within 5 s and they spill
   on the ground; otherwise they're gone. With no coins on you it pecks (8). Comes in pairs.
6. **Cave Bats** (the Bat). A swarm of 4 wheeling round you, each on its own side and height; one at a time (never two
   together) one shrieks and dives through your head (6). A punch kills one.
7. **Kraken Tentacle** (the Pirate Kit's Tentacle; the designer's own design). Only in a room with sea: it goes into
   the water (2 m out from its edge; in a narrow channel, its middle) and **lurks there unseen**. Come within 5.5 m of
   it and it **bursts out (0.3 s) and grabs you by surprise**, lifting you up in its coil; the world holds still, as in
   the Bone King's grab, and the screen counts your clicks: **6 clicks** (each a hit on it, 10) within **2.5 s** and you
   break free: it drops you on the shore unhurt and reels (2 s, hits do more). Too slow: it **slams you down on the
   shore** (25). Either way you're carried along an arc onto solid ground by where it caught you: **never into the
   sea**. Your clicks free you but **can't finish it**: the last blow is yours, punched from the shore while it reels.
   Once it's up it stays up, rooted, and when you come near again it **rears back (0.8 s) before it lunges**: step away
   and it misses. Never near it, it rises anyway after 8 s. It never flinches and can't be executed.

### Mini bosses
- **Mako, the big shark** (the designer's). Its cleaver for a while (26); then, on sand, it **dives in** (out of reach)
  and swims through the sand at you (10 m/s), only **its fin** showing; under you **the sand churns** (0.65 s: step
  off it) and it erupts, biting (32, thrown up). On boards it can't dive: it **charges you down** (12 m/s, 22, shoving
  you). Only a hard hit staggers it.
- **Captain Barbarossa**. He keeps a pistol shot off you (5–22 m). Aiming, **a red line runs from his pistol to you
  and follows you, slower than you run** (5 m/s, for 1.1 s; brighter as his shot comes); then he fires down it (28).
  Outrun the line, or get behind something. Up close, his hook sweeps wide (22). Only a hard hit staggers him.

## Its waves
Floor 6's roster is the crew (none of them on any other floor). The Gulls come in pairs and the Bats in a swarm (each
made up in place of the wave's cheapest); a Tentacle only in a room with sea (elsewhere a Cursed Crewman); at most 3
Shanty Men in a room (the rest Corsairs). The Headless
is never sent: a Cursed Crewman becomes one. Mako and Barbarossa are its mini bosses (a challenge room's too).

## With the rest of the game
- **Turns:** the Corsair and the Cursed Crewman take turns as other floors' close fighters (her hook too); the Bomber
  and Barbarossa hold their fire till it's theirs; Mako is a big one (one at a time, its swim under the sand its turn).
  The Shanty Man, the Headless, the fliers and the Tentacle go their own ways.
- **Executions:** the pirates as the Orc (the same skeleton); **the Cursed Crewman (headless or not) as floor 3's
  Skeleton** (your hand closes and it bursts into its own bones); the Gull and the Bats as the Wraith, Mako and
  Barbarossa as the Yeti. The pirates are pinned to a wall they're thrown into dead (the wall finisher; the Crewman
  bursts into its bones there too).
- **Blood:** the Cursed Crewman sheds bone chips (no blood), the Tentacle ink; **no splats on the sea** (or over any
  pit): a drop falling there is gone.
- **The Lab (F8):** floor 6 has its column again (page 2: floors 4, 5, 6); floors 7 and 8 stay in the bottom row.
- **Seen:** the hook on its rope, Barbarossa's red line, Mako's fin and the churning sand, the powder bomb (a black
  ball, its fuse sparking). The Tentacle's grab: the Bone King's click count on the screen, with its own numbers.
- **Heard:** made in code, as the other floors' until recorded ones are picked (a lute's notes, a fuse's hiss, a
  skull's rattle, a gull's cry, a shriek, the sea surging, a pistol's click and shot).

## Health

## Changed from the plan (and why)
- **The Corsair** was to parry your punches from the front: floor 4's Skull Orc already blocks from the front, so she
  has a boarding hook instead (nothing else pulls you).
- **The Shanty Man's song** quickens the crew (faster), it doesn't make their blows harder.
- **The Bomber's bombs** land and burn where they fall (no rolling), so you can read where they'll go.
- **The Bats** come 4 to a swarm (Bat swarm, 2–8 in F1), not 4–6.
- **Mako** on boards charges, no grab. Its dive is like floor 4's Cactoros going under, but it hunts you head-on with
  its fin in plain sight (the designer asked for it).
