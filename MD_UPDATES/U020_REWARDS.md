## The challenge room
- **Exactly one on every floor**, and the only fight room that leads nowhere: there's no plain fight room off the way
  any more.
- Its chest isn't there while you fight. When the last enemy dies:
  - the gates **stay shut**;
  - any of the room's keys still lying on the floor are yours (and if its mini bosses never dropped one, you're given
    it), so you can always open the chest;
  - the view leaves your eyes and goes to where the chest **rises out of the floor** (1.4 s: stones fly, the ground
    trembles, a grinding rumble), holds a moment, and comes back to you (3.2 s in all; you don't move meanwhile);
  - the gates open once you've **opened the chest and taken its item**.
- So you're never shut in for nothing, the gates also open if you have no key at all, or if you're no longer in the
  room (the lava takes you back to the floor's start).

## The boss's chest
- The boss's body bursts with nothing in it now.
- As the wooden gate opens, a chest rises out of the floor **3 m in front of it**, facing into the room (it shows in
  the gate's shot).
- It opens **without a key**, like any chest (you kneel and lift the lid): the boss's coins burst out, and its **key
  and heart** wait over it, side by side, for E.
- It doesn't hold you: the wooden gate is open.

## The teal ring
- **+25 max health** (and the health to fill it). **Mythic**: it's in mythic chests (with the Gold Daggers and the
  Grimoire), and costs **110** in the shop (the Gold Daggers' price).
- The RPG pack's teal band (Ring7). On the HUD, a teal band beside your other rings.
- It replaces the **purple ring** (+20% damage). No ring or item adds damage any more.

## The rest
- **Kill bonuses:** the snowflake and the blue potion drop from **6%** of kills (was 10%); gold ingots still 10%.
- **Executions heal:** **15** as the enemy dies; **25** for a mini boss (the Yeti, the Spider, the Blue Demon).
- **CHEST IN CURRENT ROOM !** in gold, just under the minimap, while the room you're in has a chest still to open.
- **The Spitter's gurgle** (its warning) is 65% quieter.
